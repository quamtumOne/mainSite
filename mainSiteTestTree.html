NEPO: Project Overview

The Near Earth Proximity Orbit (NEPO) project is pioneering the next generation of ultra-secure communications. By deploying a constellation of mini-satellites equipped with advanced quantum technology, NEPO aims to establish a global network resilient against current and future cyber threats.

Key innovations include:

  • Quantum Network Interface: Enabling satellites to participate in a quantum-secured network.
  • Shared Distributed Quantum Key Exchange: Generating and distributing cryptographic keys with the inherent security of quantum mechanics, making them virtually unhackable.
  • The First Quantum Phone: A revolutionary device designed for end-to-end encrypted voice and data communication, leveraging the NEPO network.

NEPO's vision is to provide unparalleled security and privacy for sensitive data exchange, catering to governments, enterprises, and individuals who demand the highest level of protection in an increasingly connected world.
